picked A · copy trimmed
"We are asking for the Presto LNA as soon as the user enters… it feels very intrusive to have an LNA without understanding why."

Where the browser exposes the permission (Chromium's local-network-access), a state that went back to "ask" brings our words back instead of a surprise.

Reading the chart and the epoch tile

picked A
"What's the unit of the vertical measurement? Why am I not going above the horizontal line?" · "Why is it measured in epochs and why can I only have 4 wins? Why can anyone close it? Close what? Tooltips would help."

The gap. The vertical axis is a proof's score, a lottery draw: a score of S comes up about once in S proofs, and a proof wins when its score reaches the bar. The chart never says the word. The epoch tile reads "wins 2 of 4" as if four were the visitor's limit; it is the whole network's round. The numbers in these boards use a bar of 38.4 so that wins are as rare as they are on a busy network.

Part two

Fixes: no options, just what it will look like

F1

One arrow per link

ExternalLink appends its own ↗ and "(opens in a new tab)"; five callers also type one: the activity row's "Etherscan" and "claim", the deposit dialog's "Etherscan", the claim dialog's "View on Etherscan", Send's "transaction". The callers lose theirs, and a test fails any ExternalLink child that ends in ↗. Three hand-rolled anchors ("Get one", "the rules, on /faq", the old tab's host) move onto the component.

F2

"Sent from Rabby Wallet" becomes "Deposit sent"

The wallet's name earns its place in the step before ("Confirm in Rabby Wallet": it tells you where to look). Once that step is done the subject is the deposit. Same change in the activity row's trail. If your objection was something else about this line, say so and I'll redraw it.

F3

The ledger's legend is a glyph key, nothing more

"final once its epoch is proven" is a rule about one line, so it moves to that line: a minted line reads "settling" until its epoch is proven and "final" after (it already says "final"; "settling" is new and carries the sentence as its tooltip). "claiming" leaves the legend too; it was never a glyph.

F4 · F7

The power slider: presets you can press, and a unit on the rate

The three labels are placed at their thread count's position on the track. On 14 cores eco (4) and balanced (7) land 66 px apart and the text collides: "eco · 4balanced · 7". They become three equal buttons under the track that set the slider (today they are inert labels); the count lives in the readout only, which also gets its unit: "20.4 proofs/min".

F5

The lighter bars are a drawing bug

Each proof's tick is stroked on its own at 55 % opacity. With 13 threads (or Presto) proofs finish in bursts; ticks that land within 2 px of each other stack, and three stacked strokes reach 91 % opacity: a bright bar out of nowhere. Both boards below hold the same proofs. The fix strokes every tick of a frame as one path, so overlap cannot add up; nothing about the data changes.

F6

The dialog's horizontal scrollbar

The account dialog sets overflow-y:auto so a tall checklist can scroll. CSS then computes overflow-x to auto as well, and any child a pixel too wide (a nowrap right column, a long address) raises a scrollbar. Moderate confidence on which child it is; I'll reproduce it in Playwright first. The fix is the same either way: overflow-x:clip on the dialog, and the stepper's right column may shrink and wrap instead of forcing its width. D3's new stepper carries the second half.

F8

Settings says "13 threads" once

The tile's corner and the slider's own label row print the same number 40 px apart. The corner goes; the row stays. Drawn in D4's Settings boards, which replace the whole tile.

F9

The zero address is a placeholder that leaked

A deposit this browser didn't make itself (another device, or a restored journal) is found by matching its secret on-chain. The sender isn't part of that match, so landing.ts writes twenty zero bytes into ethAddress and whoOf prints them. The row stops printing an address it doesn't have; D8 decides what it says instead ("found on Ethereum" in option A). The field becomes optional in the journal rather than zero-filled, so nothing else can print it by accident.
