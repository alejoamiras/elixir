## What the plan said and what shipped differently

- **`Tip`'s trigger is a `<button>`, not a focusable `<span>`**: Biome's `noNoninteractiveTabindex` refuses
  `tabIndex` on a span, and radix's own trigger is a button anyway. It is styled to inherit font and colour, so it
  reads as the dotted word of the boards.
- **The boards give one tooltip sentence (the epoch's), and mark six labels as dotted.** The other five are
  written here from the rules the page already reads (`N`, `T_MAX`, the ×4 clamp), in `epochTips(rules)`:
  nothing in them is a number the chain does not back.
- **The grid's rows depend on what stands above the cockpit.** `xl:grid-rows-[auto_auto_1fr]` is right for the bare
  cockpit; a notice or the upgrade card is one more row, and the `1fr` must stay on the *last* left row or the
  slack opens a gap between the KPIs and the ledger. Three literal class strings indexed by the count of cards
  above (Tailwind needs the literals in source); a spec holds the notice case.
- **Below `xl` the right column dissolves (`contents`)**: between `md` and `xl` the rail stands beside the balance
  with the ledger under it ("the balance over the ledger", §3.4); on a phone the balance is second, right under
  the loop. The boards draw only the wide frame; the two narrower ones are the plan's sentence, read as above.
- **jsdom has no `ResizeObserver`** and radix's popper measures with it: a no-op in `ui`'s and `web-miner`'s
  vitest setup, since every spec that mounts Mine now mounts tips. The specs assert content and state, never
  placement; placement is the e2e's.
- The mint line lives in its own component (`MintLine`): inside `BalanceCard` its ternary took the function one
  over the cognitive budget.

## Smaller things

- The worktree guard refuses a long inline `bun -e` with helper closures ("too complex to verify"); the same edit
  as a script file in the scratchpad runs. A refused command changes nothing — check before building on it.
- A test title with an ASCII apostrophe inside a single-quoted string generated by a script breaks the file's
  parse; the repo's titles use the typographic one.
