What happens to your coins, in plain words.

Mining

What is the work?

Your browser proves a fixed Noir circuit; the proof is the work. Its score is a hash of the whole proof, and a score below the bar wins 4 tYACA, minted privately to an account only you hold.

What does the chain see?

A claim writes a nullifier, a note hash and a counter. Not who claimed, not how much they hold, not how many proofs it took.

Can the rules change?

The contracts are immutable. What can change is Aztec itself: a new version of the rollup replaces the old one, and that is what the next section is about.

When Aztec upgrades

Aztec’s Alpha rollups do not carry balances to the next version. Yacana moves yours through Ethereum in six steps; you act at step 2 and step 6.

1 · on v5You mine on V5.Rewards are private notes in your account.
2 · send aheadYou send it ahead.Your balance leaves V5 with a secret only your account knows.
3 · ethereumHeld on Ethereum.Proven with its epoch, out of V5’s reach. The amount is visible there, nothing else.
4 · the flipV6 becomes canonical.Mining on V5 ends. Yacana then opens its V6 contract and registers it.
5 · forwardedThe relayer forwards it.One Ethereum transaction, which anyone may send.
6 · landsIt lands in your account.You sign in on V6 with the same passkey; the page claims it for you.
What do I do?

When an upgrade is announced, Yacana shows one button: Send ahead. Press it before the upgrade, and let the page send ahead what you mine after that. Then, once V6 is live, sign in at yacana.network with the same passkey or words; your balance lands there.

What if I miss it?

After the upgrade, mining on V5 ends: its contract refuses every claim from then on. But V5 keeps proving epochs for hours or days, without notice of when it stops, and while it does the old app at v5.yacana.network still lets you send ahead what you already hold: a bet that V5 proves one more epoch, against the sure loss of leaving it. A send V5 never proves is undone back onto V5. Anything still on V5 when it stops is lost.

What if Yacana never opens V6?

Your commitment is proven and held on Ethereum, not on any Yacana contract. Thirty days after the upgrade, a commitment that could not be forwarded can be redeemed on Ethereum as YACA by the account that made it.

Is it the same account on V6?

Same passkey or words, same keys; a new address, because Aztec derives addresses from the account contract of each version. What you sent ahead lands under the new address; nothing on V5 knows it.

What is public?

The amount, on Ethereum, while it waits. Someone watching could pair a commitment with an arrival of the same amount on V6; round amounts blend in. Nothing names your account.

What if the relayer is slow?

Your commitment is safe on Ethereum. Forwarding is one transaction that anyone may send; after an hour the app offers to let you send it yourself. After 30 days without a V6 to forward to, you may redeem it as YACA on Ethereum instead.

To Ethereum and back

Can I hold it on Ethereum?

Yes: To Ethereum in the wallet burns tYACA here and mints YACA, an ERC-20, to an address you choose. The amount and the address are public there.

Can I bring it back?

Yes: Deposit from Ethereum burns YACA there and mints it privately here, to your account, by itself.

Who can stop it?

The operators (a 2-of-3 multisig) can pause the bridge for up to 30 days at a time, 60 in total per version, and register each new version once. They cannot redirect a commitment or keep an exit from landing once a pause lifts.
