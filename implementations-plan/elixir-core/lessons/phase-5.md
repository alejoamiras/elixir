# Phase 5 lessons — testnet deploy + soak (homelab, 2026-09-04)

- **Secret exposure on the first deploy attempt.** The deployer secret in the owner's env has no `0x` prefix; `Fr.fromString` rejected it and its error message *echoes the input*, so the raw secret landed in the deploy log (gitignored `*.log`) and in the agent's captured output. The log and the captured task outputs were deleted immediately; nothing was committed or pushed. A second attempt with the prefix normalised failed the same way one step later: the value is a 32-byte key above the BN254 modulus, and `Fr`'s range error echoes the value too. The CLI now validates the variable with a regex and reduces it into the field with `Fr.fromBufferReduce` before any library sees it (a fixed, deterministic mapping of the owner's key to one deployer account), and throws messages without the value. **The owner should rotate the testnet deployer secret** (testnet-only key; the mainnet secret was never read).
- Testnet node at deploy time: `5.2.0-nightly.20260815` (node version), L1 chain 11155111, rollup version 1821665230; local toolchain 5.2.0 (`bb 5.2.0-nightly.20260807`). The 5.2.0 client, contracts and client-side proofs were accepted by that node (deploy of three txs, then claims), so the nightly suffix is not a protocol break.
- Deployment (third attempt, 01:27 UTC): miner `0x1e57c929…90665f`, token `0x2e4c2883…154fa3`, deployer `0x2c7a1312…e3c54b` — `docs/deployments.md`, `deployments/testnet.json`.
- **A public revert bricks the claimant's wallet (found 02:11 UTC).** Miner A's claim in epoch 8 reverted in public at block 68585 (miner B had closed the epoch one block earlier — the expected stale race, fee paid). Every later claim from A was refused at simulation with "Nullifier read request at index 5 is reading an unknown nullifier: 0x0e70…" — the *same* nullifier in epochs 9 and 10, minutes apart, while miner B (a separate wallet) kept claiming. The read request is the token's constrained note delivery (`MessageDelivery::onchain_constrained`): message `i > 0` asserts that message `i − 1`'s sequence nullifier exists on chain. The reverted tx's private effects — including its sequence nullifier — were discarded by the chain, but the PXE had already advanced its local sequence, so every following claim asserts a nullifier that never landed. First read as a transient anchor-block race (a retry was added, then removed once the cause was clear), it is a PXE desync with a defined end: the PXE (`@aztec/pxe/dest/tagging/sender_sync/*`) chooses the next tag index as highest *pending* + 1 and only reconciles a reverted tx's pending indexes (`finalizePendingIndexesOfAPartiallyRevertedTx`) once its receipt is **FINALIZED** (`resolvePendingTxs` → `isFinalized`), i.e. proven and settled on L1 — tens of minutes on the testnet. Until then the claimant cannot deliver another note to itself through this token, so it cannot claim. Miner A was replaced at 02:16 by `miner-a2` (fresh ephemeral wallet, remaining schedule half → pause → full) so the soak keeps two miners. **This is the most important Phase 5 finding**: stale reverts are a normal part of the mechanism (§2.4), so a miner must recover from them; see the A3 codex round-2 notes and the roadmap for the recovery design in the web miner.
- Also seen: `stale-before-send` when the other miner closed the epoch between the win and the send (no fee, by design); a claim reverted in public at 1 351 740 946 857 519 424 FJ-wei vs ≈ 1.55–2.00 × 10^18 for successes (testnet gas prices; the closer pays most).
- **Soak result (`docs/soak-report.md`, 01:28–03:28 UTC, exactly the 2 h cap; 21 epochs closed, so the cap came before the 24-epoch target).** Median epoch 288 s against the 300 s target with 11–16 threads of native proving (min 144 s under the combined hashrate peak, max 1296 s for the epoch nobody mined until the roll); retargets ×0.48–×1.92 on count closes and exactly ×4.00 on the roll; 84 accepted claims across three miner runs (A 31 before its revert, B 13, A2 40 → 160 tELX in one account), 1 public revert, 6 refusals/discards (3 of them A's blocked-delivery refusals, 3 stale winners discarded before send), 1 roll. Difficulty tracked the schedule: 16 → 54 at the 16-thread peak, down to 4.7 after the pause, back to 33 at full threads. The initial target was ≈ 3× too easy for native proving (first epoch 288 s only because the claims' own proving time paced it); the retarget needed two epochs to settle. Native W proof median 1.78 s (contention with the E2E runs and the second miner).
- Soak design (owner: testnet profile, ≤ 2 h, hashrate varied): miner A (`soak.ts --hours 2 --schedule full,half,pause,full`, 30-minute steps, 11 → 5 → 0 → 11 threads, native bb) from 01:28 UTC; miner B (`--hours 1.5 --schedule pause,full,pause`) from 01:30 UTC, so the combined hashrate goes 11 → 16 → 0 → 11 threads and the zero step (02:28–02:58) lasts longer than `T_MAX` = 20 min to exercise `roll()`. Native W proof ≈ 1.6 s at 11 threads; the first testnet claim was mined 17 s after its winning proof (block 68534, fee 1.66 FJ-wei × 10^18 at testnet gas prices).
